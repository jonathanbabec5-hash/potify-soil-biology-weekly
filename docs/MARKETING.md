# Soil Biology Weekly marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Living-soil growers and readers of From Seed to Smoke. **Offer.** Translate one observable living-soil question into a sourced, readable Friday brief.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Use a short field note to invite one follow-up observation and link the related grow episode. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Submit one reviewed field question. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 grounded reader questions in four issues. |

### Sales test

After four reviewed issues, offer one free digest page and collect three price-confirmed requests before producing a paid seasonal edition. Test interest in a seasonal printed field-notes digest when four reviewed issues exist.

### Measurement and decision

Use `soil-biology-weekly-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** This release provides the editorial starter. A complete reviewed issue archive was not located in the selected sources. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
