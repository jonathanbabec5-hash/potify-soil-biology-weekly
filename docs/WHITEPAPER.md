# Soil Biology Weekly
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Editorial research kit**

### Goal and audience

Translate one observable living-soil question into a sourced, readable Friday brief. The immediate audience is living-soil growers and readers of From Seed to Smoke.

### Design and implementation

A structured issue template links a grower's observation, a published source and the limits of the interpretation. Readers can compare it with the next dated observation. Begin with a dated observation and keep plausible interpretation separate from the observed condition. A follow-up is most valuable when it could disconfirm the first impression.

### What a contributor can establish

The commands below ran in a clean test context using local files and fixtures. Tie each outside source to the exact statement it supports. Test whether readers understand what a weekly note can and cannot establish.

### Evidence and outstanding work

This release provides the editorial starter. A complete reviewed issue archive was not located in the selected sources. The next acceptance case is concrete. Run an editorial review of one brief and verify that observation and interpretation can be told apart.

### Place in POTIFY

Test interest in a seasonal printed field-notes digest when four reviewed issues exist. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
